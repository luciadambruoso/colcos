The study on regional television

Lucia D’Ambruoso was interviewed about the study on That’s TV Scotland, broadcast on 29 February 2024.

The interview covered what community partners found — the convergence of financial stress with the rising availability and acceptability of smoking products, and the gap in awareness of the free cessation support that already exists locally.
