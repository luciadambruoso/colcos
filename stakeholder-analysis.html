Evidence  ·  03   Stakeholder analysis

“The individual is a grain of sand”

Services did not appear to be immediately accessible in the under-served communities where we worked. We mapped relationships and interactions between various actors and institutions, and explored how individual willpower needs to be met with accessible services. Combined with macro-level factors and forces, these constrain people’s agency for cessation.

The “big players” in smoking and smoking cessation were identified in the Venn diagram — the bigger circles, highlighted in yellow. These included the tobacco and e-cigarette manufacturers, whose main objective is to profit from selling tobacco and related products. Overlapping with the tobacco companies, the next big player was Government, seen as the institution with the power to limit the affordability of cigarettes, for example by increasing tax.

In terms of services, including smoking cessation, peer support groups were seen as non-existent — the green circles. Online information was discussed as a key source and a potential strategy for quitting: “you go online for all information”, and everyone has access. But there were positives and negatives to that. Help was available, and the internet also provides ease of access to e-cigarettes.

“We can have all the willpower but we need the support of other elements to be successful.”
Community participant  ·  stakeholder analysis workshop  ·  2023

Participants expressed that the individual is a grain of sand compared with the bigger players who influence policy, marketing and the availability of tobacco and related products. Individuals need support to be successful.
