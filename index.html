The problem statement

“No means to break the cycle.”

Community researchers mapped what the statistics could not reach: financial stress rising at the same moment that smoking and vaping products became cheaper, closer and easier to buy. Working with the Problem Tree tool, they traced the causes and consequences of smoking as they are actually lived — and named the loop that keeps people in it.

How the study worked

Data and dialogue

Community experts and service providers built evidence together, in facilitated learning spaces, then analysed it together. Six forms of evidence came out of it.

Convene

Community members in under-served communities became co-researchers, not subjects, and set the local health priorities themselves.

About the study

Generate

Problem trees, stakeholder maps, photovoice and rapid evidence reviews, alongside routine and administrative statistics.
