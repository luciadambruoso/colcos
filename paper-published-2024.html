Cost of Living/Cost of Smoking published in Social Policy & Administration

The demonstration study is published, peer reviewed and open access, in Social Policy & Administration.

D’Ambruoso L, Stephen A, Cowan E, Innocent W, Marathia E, Littlejohn C, Duffy S, and the Cost of Living/Cost of Smoking Learning Partnership. Cost of Living/Cost of Smoking: A Demonstration Study of Cooperative Action Learning to Understand and Address Smoking in Deprived Communities Within the Cost-of-Living Crisis. Social Policy & Administration, 2024.

Nine community-based participants took part in eight workshops as co-researchers, collecting and arranging new data and evidence. Three further workshops with service providers analysed and interpreted that data, appraised local action and reflected on the process. Community partners identified a convergence between increased stress owing to the crisis and the increased availability, affordability and acceptability of tobacco-related products, particularly e-cigarettes — compounded by a lack of awareness of the cessation services that already exist.
