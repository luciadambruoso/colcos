Evidence  ·  01   Problem tree

“No means to break the cycle”

We mapped the convergence of increased stress from the financial crisis and increased availability of smoking products in communities. We used the Problem Tree tool to understand and unpack the topic from different perspectives, and to systematise subjective experience into collective forms of knowledge.
