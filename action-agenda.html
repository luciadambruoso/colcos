Action Agenda

The future we want

Our action reflection is collectively developed to represent moving towards a desired goal via a series of interconnected steps and events. We reviewed alternative courses of action and specified and appraised interventions, their feasibility and acceptability, and potential barriers including cultural factors.

Three priorities were agreed with the health authority.

01

Targeting root causes

Cessation support has to recognise that smoking is, for some people, the only stress relief they have. Healthy alternatives to de-stress were identified: CBT, meditation, understanding your own mind.

02

Inclusive access

Visibility of campaigns. There was frustration in the room: how can people access a service if they do not know it exists?

03

Incentivising

Money saved for a holiday rather than for groceries or energy. ‘Free’ matters enormously for people who are struggling.
