Going forward: implications for policy and practice

We returned to NHS Grampian to present the completed study and to deliberate over what it means for policy and practice, at a meeting of the Grampian Engagement Network on 4 December 2024.

By this point the study had been published, the action agenda had been agreed, and the health improvement team had incorporated community intelligence into new campaigns. The discussion was therefore less about findings and more about what happens next: whether cooperative action learning can be embedded as a routine function rather than run as a project, and which service areas it might serve beyond smoking.
