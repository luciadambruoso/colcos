What the approach offers

Methods you can take somewhere else

Evidence for under-served populations

Few interventions specifically target populations living in deprived social circumstances. We analysed the costs of smoking during the cost-of-living crisis in under-served communities in rural North-East Scotland, gathering and analysing different forms of evidence to give organisations something to plan with.

Data science and visualisation

Tobacco consumption is socially patterned. We use data to understand the prevalence of smoking, the associated health risks, and the root causes of smoking and smoking-related illness — with a focus on under-served communities.

Practical tools

A set of tools, techniques and practical skills to convene community groups and understand local health priorities; to understand health issues from different perspectives and generate new data on them; and to facilitate and monitor action in clinics, communities and other public services.

Authentic community engagement

Community participation is known to be necessary to achieve health for all, but practitioner knowledge of how to operationalise the concept is limited. We developed methods and tools to build community power in public health in a rural area of North-East Scotland.
